Our Story

Founded in 2020, Koka (Kold Caffeine) was born from a passion for exceptional cold brew coffee. Our journey began in a small garage in Portland, where our founder Emily Chen spent countless nights perfecting the cold brew process.

What sets us apart is our commitment to quality and innovation. We source only the finest single-origin beans, use filtered water, and steep our cold brew for a full 24 hours to extract the perfect flavor profile – rich, smooth, and never bitter.

Today, with multiple locations across the city, we remain dedicated to our original mission: serving the best cold brew coffee while creating a warm, welcoming space for our community.
