Brewing Perfection Since 2015

How It All Began

Koka Coffee was born from a simple yet profound love for exceptional coffee. Our founder, Emma Chen, spent years traveling across coffee-growing regions, from the highlands of Ethiopia to the mountains of Colombia, developing a deep appreciation for the craft of coffee making.

What started as a small cart in downtown Seattle has grown into a beloved community space where quality coffee, meaningful connections, and sustainability come together.

Our Coffee Philosophy

At Koka, we believe that great coffee is an art form. We meticulously source our beans directly from farmers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability. Each batch is roasted in small quantities to ensure peak flavor and freshness.

Our baristas are trained in the science and craft of coffee preparation, ensuring that every cup served meets our exacting standards. We're not just selling coffee – we're sharing an experience crafted with care and expertise.

Our Core Values

Sustainability

We are committed to environmentally responsible practices, from sourcing to serving. Our packaging is compostable, and we actively work to reduce our carbon footprint.

Quality

We never compromise on quality. From bean selection to brewing techniques, every step is executed with precision and care to deliver an exceptional coffee experience.

Community

Koka is more than a coffee shop – it's a gathering place. We foster connections among our customers and give back to the communities that support us.
